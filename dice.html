You and Stepz will both roll 5 dice. The highest "Poker Hand" wins. Use Auto Roll for speed or Roll Single for suspense!

3. Full House 3 match + 2 match

5. Three of a Kind 3 matching dice
6. Two Pair 2 pairs of matching dice
7. One Pair 1 pair of matching dice
8. High Card Highest single die
